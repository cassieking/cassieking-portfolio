## Problem Statement

Cassie is an experienced hospitality professional (4+ years, progressive responsibility from host → head server → bar manager → server/bartender/trainer at an upscale steakhouse) who is reaching burnout from the **physical demands of floor-level service work**. The schedule (nights/weekends) is tolerable. The grind is not.

She has the skills, certifications, and track record for management — she's already managed a bar, trained staff, and coordinated large-party service at a high-end Brazilian steakhouse. But her **job titles** don't yet reflect that trajectory, and she hasn't been strategically building credentials to cross into adjacent industries.

The gap is not skill — it's positioning and credentialing.

---

## Solution

Execute a **2–6 month strategic career transition** from floor-level hospitality work into a supervisory or management role across one of three lanes:

| Lane | Description | Readiness |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **A — Restaurant Management** | AGM, FOH Manager, Restaurant Manager at an upscale property | Ready now |
| **B — Broader Hospitality** | F&B Manager, Banquet Manager, Conference Services Manager at hotels/resorts | Ready with CHS certification |
| **C — Events & Corporate Ops** | Event Manager, Venue Manager, Office Manager at convention centers, venues, or corporate offices | Ready with Google PM Certificate + Cvent |

The transition is supported by:
1. A prioritized list of **29 target job titles** with salary ranges and search terms
2. A **tiered certification roadmap** (6 certifications across 3 tiers, ordered by ROI)
3. A **resume repositioning strategy** that leads with operations leadership, not server/bartender work
4. A **targeted employer list** for the San Antonio market

## Implementation Decisions

### Job Title Selection
- **29 titles** identified across 3 lanes and 5 sub-categories (Lane A, Lane B, Lane C1 Events, Lane C2 Corporate)
- Each title vetted against all 4 hard constraints: no sales, no floor grind, $50K–$70K range, San Antonio/Texas availability
- Titles with potential sales components (e.g., Catering Manager) are flagged with explicit warnings to read job descriptions carefully
- Full title list, definitions, salary ranges, and search terms documented in [job_titles_and_training.md](file:///Users/cassieking/.gemini/antigravity/brain/ffbfb2a8-bd5b-4510-aa73-63537cb726a4/job_titles_and_training.md)

### Search Strategy
- Exact boolean search strings provided for each lane, designed for Indeed, LinkedIn, and ZipRecruiter
- Negative keywords included to filter out irrelevant results (e.g., `-"fast food"`, `-nightclub`, `-warehouse`)
- Employer-specific targeting lists for San Antonio market: 8 hotel properties, 10 event/corporate employers

### Resume Positioning
- **Lead with:** Progressive management experience (4+ years), bar management title, team development, operational leadership
- **Credential block:** TABC + Food Handler + Food Manager + (incoming CHS and Google PM Certificate)
- **Differentiator hook:** International forensics tournament runner-up — use as a "tell me more" conversation starter
- **Do not lead with:** Server/bartender titles. These are supporting evidence of hands-on expertise, not the headline.
- **Communications major:** Position as formal training in structured communication, not "dropped out"

### Credential Building (Tiered Roadmap)

**Tier 1 — Months 1–2 (do simultaneously):**
- AHLEI Certified Hospitality Supervisor (CHS): ~$270–$450, 2–4 weeks study, validates supervisory experience for Lanes A & B
- Google Project Management Certificate (Coursera): ~$150–$300 total ($49/mo), 2–6 months, foundational for Lanes B & C

**Tier 2 — Months 2–4:**
- Cvent Supplier Certification: Free, ~10 hours, key differentiator for event roles
- AHLEI Certified Hospitality Manager (CHM): ~$400–$600, 4–6 weeks study, positions for AGM/Restaurant Manager

**Tier 3 — Months 4–6+:**
- Certified Meeting Professional (CMP): ~$400–$500 exam, requires 3 years event experience — begin tracking hours now
- Hotel brand management programs: Research Marriott Voyage, Hilton Elevator, Hyatt programs for experienced professionals

### Application Priority Order
1. **Lane A** (Restaurant Management) — resume wins today, apply immediately
2. **Lane B** (Hotel F&B/Banquet/Conference) — apply after CHS certification
3. **Lane C1** (Event venues/convention centers) — apply with Google PM Certificate in progress + Cvent certification
4. **Lane C2** (Corporate/facilities operations) — apply after Google PM Certificate completion

### Constraints (Non-Negotiable)
- No role where personal performance is evaluated against revenue, expansion, upsell, or retention dollar targets
- No role where the day-to-day is primarily physical labor (carrying trays, running food, standing behind a bar all shift)
- San Antonio preferred; Austin, Dallas, Houston acceptable; no relocation outside Texas
- $50K–$70K target compensation range
- Hospitality schedule (nights/weekends) is acceptable if the role is supervisory

## Testing Decisions

Every opportunity Cassie considers should be evaluated against the following **acceptance criteria**:

### The Role Filter (apply to every job posting)
| Test | Pass | Fail |
|------|------|------|
| **Sales test** | No revenue/sales targets in job description or performance reviews | Description mentions quotas, pipeline, revenue targets, upselling KPIs |
| **Grind test** | Duties are supervisory: scheduling, managing staff, coordinating operations, solving problems | Duties are execution: serving tables, bartending shifts, carrying trays, running food |
| **Comp test** | Listed salary or range includes $50K+ (or is negotiable/unlisted at a level-appropriate title) | Listed max salary is below $45K with no room |
| **Location test** | Role is in San Antonio or another Texas metro | Role requires relocation outside Texas |

### The Interview Filter (apply during conversations with employers)
- "What does a typical day look like?" — listen for whether the answer is supervising or doing
- "How is performance evaluated?" — listen for revenue targets or sales metrics
- "What happened to the last person in this role?" — listen for burnout signals
- "Is there a path to [next title]?" — assess growth potential

### Credential Validation
- Track certification completion dates
- Update resume and LinkedIn within 48 hours of earning each credential
- Measure: applications sent per week, response rate before vs. after credential additions

## Further Notes

### San Antonio Market Advantage
San Antonio is an underrated market for this transition. The city has:
- A massive hotel/resort corridor (Riverwalk, La Cantera, Hill Country resorts)
- The Henry B. González Convention Center (one of the largest in TX)
- A strong military/corporate presence (USAA, Valero, H-E-B HQ) with facilities/operations needs
- A growing food & beverage scene with upscale properties that promote from within

### The Fogo Leverage Play
Cassie is currently at Fogo de Chao — a national brand with internal promotion paths. Before looking externally, it's worth having a direct conversation with the GM about the timeline to a Service Manager or AGM promotion. If Fogo can offer that path in 3–6 months, it may be the lowest-risk Lane A move available. If not, the external search is well-defined.

### The Speech Competition as a Secret Weapon
Runner-up at an international forensics tournament in Germany is a genuinely unusual credential in hospitality management. Most candidates at the AGM/F&B Manager level cannot demonstrate elite-level communication skills with this kind of proof point. Use it in:
- Resume summary (one line)
- Cover letter opening (hook)
- Interview responses about leadership and communication
- LinkedIn headline or featured section
