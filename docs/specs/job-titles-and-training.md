# Job Titles & Training Roadmap — Cassie King

> The result of a grilling session to sharpen job descriptions, identify the best titles to search for, and map out a training plan. Everything below is filtered through the constraints we established: no sales, no floor grind, $50K–$70K target, San Antonio preferred (Texas OK), and a 2–6 month strategic timeline.

---

## Lane A — Restaurant Management

**Your strongest lane.** You already have the experience, the certifications, and a current role at an upscale property. These are the roles where your resume wins *today*.

| Job Title | What It Actually Means | SA Salary Range | Confidence |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Assistant General Manager (AGM)** | Right hand to the GM. Runs the floor, handles scheduling, manages FOH staff, steps in for the GM. This is the #1 target. | $50K–$65K | 🟢 High |
| **Front of House Manager (FOH Manager)** | Owns the dining room operation — staffing, guest experience, service standards, training. Less admin than AGM. | $48K–$60K | 🟢 High |
| **Restaurant Manager** | Sometimes means AGM, sometimes means GM at a smaller place. Read the description carefully. | $50K–$70K | 🟢 High |
| **Dining Room Manager** | Common at upscale/fine dining. Runs the dining room specifically, not bar or kitchen. Your Fogo experience maps perfectly. | $48K–$62K | 🟢 High |
| **Service Manager** | Fogo and similar steakhouse groups use this title. Basically FOH Manager with a hospitality-brand spin. | $48K–$60K | 🟢 High |
| **Beverage Manager / Beverage Director** | Owns the bar program — cocktail menus, wine lists, inventory, bar staff. Your bartending + bar manager experience qualifies you. | $50K–$65K | 🟡 Medium |
| **Banquet Manager (restaurant)** | Manages private dining and large party events within a restaurant. Hits your "orchestrating big moments" energy. | $45K–$58K | 🟡 Medium |

| **Banquet Manager (hotel)** | Manages all banquet and event operations — weddings, conferences, galas. Your large-party coordination + operations strengths shine here. | $48K–$62K | 🟢 High |

| **Conference Services Manager** | Hotel-side coordinator for meetings and conferences. Works with clients pre-event, manages setup/execution. | $48K–$62K | 🟢 High |

| **Catering Manager** | Coordinates catering for events — menus, staffing, logistics. ⚠️ *Some catering manager roles have sales targets — read descriptions carefully.* | $48K–$62K | 🟡 Watch for sales |

## Training & Certification Roadmap

Ordered by **impact-per-dollar** — what will move the needle most for the least time and money.

### Tier 1: Do These First (Months 1–2)

| Certification | Why | Cost | Time | Best For |
|--------------|-----|------|------|----------|
| **Google Project Management Certificate** (Coursera) | Teaches you the vocabulary and frameworks that operations/event/facilities roles expect. Gives you a portfolio of project artifacts. Recognized by major employers. Bridges the "no degree" gap. | ~$150–$300 ($49/mo) | 3–6 months (can accelerate to 2) | Lane C (all roles), Lane B (conference/banquet manager) |
| **AHLEI Certified Hospitality Supervisor (CHS)** | Industry-standard certification that validates your supervisory experience. You already qualify based on your Fogo trainer role. Quick win. | ~$270–$450 | 2–4 weeks of study | Lane A, Lane B |

### Tier 2: Do These Next (Months 2–4)

| Certification | Why | Cost | Time | Best For |
|--------------|-----|------|------|----------|
| **Cvent Supplier Certification** (free) | Cvent is the dominant event management software. Having this on your resume for any event coordinator/manager role is a significant edge. It's free. | Free | ~10 hours | Lane B (banquet/conference), Lane C (events) |
| **AHLEI Certified Hospitality Manager (CHM)** | The next level up from CHS. Covers financial management, revenue management, and leadership at the GM level. Positions you for AGM/Restaurant Manager. | ~$400–$600 | 4–6 weeks of study | Lane A, Lane B |

### Tier 3: Build Toward These (Months 4–6+)

| Certification | Why | Cost | Time | Best For |
|--------------|-----|------|------|----------|
| **Certified Meeting Professional (CMP)** | The gold standard for the events industry. Requires 3 years of event industry experience (which you're building). Start tracking your event hours now. | ~$400–$500 (exam) | 3+ months study | Lane C (events) — long game |
| **Hotel Brand Management Programs** | Marriott, Hilton, and Hyatt all have management development programs. Some accept experienced hospitality professionals without degrees. Worth exploring if Lane B is the priority. | Employer-paid | Varies | Lane B |

> [!TIP]
> **Quick win strategy:** Get the CHS ($270, 2 weeks) and start the Google PM Certificate ($49/mo) *simultaneously*. Within 60 days you'll have one new certification in hand and another in progress. That's enough to start applying to the top-tier roles while continuing to build credentials.
