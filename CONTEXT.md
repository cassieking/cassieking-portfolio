# Job Search — Domain Glossary

## Career Lanes

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Lane A — Restaurant Management** | Moving up within restaurants to management-level roles (AGM, FOH Manager, Restaurant Manager). Not staying at server/bartender level — the goal is to get off the floor and into leadership. |
| **Lane B — Broader Hospitality** | Roles outside restaurants but still in the hospitality industry: hotels, resorts, event venues. Examples: Guest Services Supervisor, F&B Supervisor, Banquet Captain. |
| **Lane C — Non-Hospitality Customer-Facing** | Customer-facing roles outside the restaurant/hotel industry. Narrowed to two environments: (1) Event venues, convention centers, and wedding/conference venues; (2) Corporate office operations, facilities management, and administrative leadership. No property management, healthcare, or senior living. |

## Hard Constraints

| Constraint | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| **No Sales — Hard Line** | Any role where personal performance is evaluated against revenue, expansion, upsell, or retention dollar targets is excluded. This rules out Account Executive, BDR, Customer Success Manager (with expansion targets), and relationship-based Account Manager. Soft upselling in a hospitality context (wine pairings, dessert suggestions) is fine — that's service, not sales. |
| **No Floor Grind** | The physical demands of day-to-day server/bartender work are the primary burnout trigger. The schedule (nights/weekends) is a secondary factor but tolerable if the role is supervisory. The key test: "Am I directing the operation or doing the grunt work?" If the answer is grunt work, it's a no. |
| **Location** | San Antonio, TX preferred. Open to other Texas cities (Austin, Dallas, Houston). Not open to relocation outside Texas. |
| **Compensation** | Target range $50K–$70K. This is achievable for Lane A (restaurant management at upscale properties), stretches for some Lane B roles (hotel F&B supervisor may start lower, F&B Manager/Banquet Manager can hit it), and requires targeting mid-level Lane C roles (not entry-level coordinator). |
